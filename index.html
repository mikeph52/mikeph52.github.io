02 — Experience

Where I've worked

Bioinformatician

March 2026 — Present

Institute of Marine Biology, Biotechnology and Aquaculture, Hellenic Centre for Marine Research

Worked primarily on developing Nessie, a workflow for de novo genome assembly (Github:https://github.com/ mikeph52/nessie). Genome assemblies generated:

  • Ziphius cavirostris
  • Stenella coeruleoalba
  • Hippospongia communis

Another area of focus is Genome decontamination, including the identification and removal of contaminant sequences from genome assemblies.

Undergraduate Thesis

2024 — 2025

LBBA, Hellenic Mediterranean University

Completed my thesis, titled “Study of the Antimicrobial Properties of Indigenous Yeasts Against Known Plant Pathogenic Bacteria”, under the supervision of Assistant Professor Emmanouil Trantas, Department of Agriculture, HMU. The study investigated the antimicrobial activity of indigenous yeast strains against known plant pathogenic bacteria using microbiological assays, including the cotton swab assay and double-layer assay.

05 — About

A little more context

I’m a bioinformatician and software developer interested in genomics, evolution, and metagenomics. Most of my work revolves around genome assembly, genome decontamination, and building bioinformatics tools that make working with biological data a little easier. I’m currently doing my MSc at the University of Crete, and I’m hoping to continue with a PhD and keep exploring the world of genomics & biology.
